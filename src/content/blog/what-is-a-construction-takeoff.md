In the construction industry, accurate project cost estimation begins with a **construction takeoff** — the process of measuring and quantifying all materials needed for a project directly from blueprints or plans.

## Why Takeoffs Matter

A missed measurement or an incorrect material count can lead to project overruns, delays, and lost profit. Professional takeoffs eliminate guesswork and give contractors a data-backed foundation for bidding.

## What Does a Takeoff Include?

A complete takeoff typically covers:

- **Concrete and masonry** — volume and square footage
- **Framing lumber** — linear feet and board counts
- **Roofing materials** — shingles, underlayment, and flashing
- **Mechanical, electrical, and plumbing** — fixture and pipe counts
- **Finishes** — flooring, drywall, and paint coverage
